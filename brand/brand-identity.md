# Brand Identity

Who 917 Ventures is, how the brand presents itself, and the principles every
917 Ventures digital product should express. Source of truth: the live site at
https://917ventures.com (analyzed 2026-10-22).

Evidence labels used throughout this skill:

- **Verified** — observed directly on 917ventures.com (markup, CSS, assets, or rendered pages).
- **Proposed** — a recommendation that extends the brand to products the website does not cover (dashboards, admin portals, reports). Follow it unless a project owner overrides it.

## Who the brand is (Verified)

- **Name:** 917Ventures (written as one word on the site, with "917V" as the short form, e.g. "917V Life").
- **Positioning:** "One of the largest venture builders in Southeast Asia" (hero headline). The meta description reads: "917Ventures is the largest corporate venture builder that ideates, launches, accelerates, and scales new business ideas that uplift the lives of Filipinos."
- **Mission language:** "building futures", "uplift the lives of Filipinos and beyond", "shaping the digital landscape", "Fostering Inclusion and Sustainability in All Aspects of Life".
- **Portfolio:** GCash, m360, AdSpark, DeepSea, Inquiro, RUSH, Concati, Pettr, KodeGo, Gogoro, KonsultaMD, Evro. Portfolio tiles carry "EXITED" or "SPUN IN" ribbons.
- **Contact:** hello@917ventures.com; 18th Floor, W Fifth, 5th Ave BGC, Taguig City, Philippines.
- **Social:** Facebook (917Ventures), Instagram (917venturesph), LinkedIn (917-ventures).
- **Flagship event:** "Velocity" (a sub-brand with its own page and a purple/violet visual treatment; see [color-palette.md](color-palette.md#velocity-sub-brand-palette-verified)).

## Personality (Verified from tone and visuals)

| Trait | How the site expresses it |
|---|---|
| Confident, institutional | Large uppercase Unbounded headlines in brand blue; big impact numbers ("46+ Ideas vetted"). |
| Optimistic, forward-looking | Space, globe and "astronaut" imagery; dot-grid world map on a deep navy hero; language about building futures. |
| Human and local | Testimonials from real Filipino customers and graduates with photos and names; Philippines-focused copy. |
| Clean and corporate-modern | Generous white space, white cards with soft shadows, a single blue accent, restrained motion. |

## Visual identity at a glance (Verified)

- **Light-first UI.** White page backgrounds, near-black text (#040815), a single primary blue (#407DC3). A deep navy (#142741) is used for the hero only. There is no dark theme on the site.
- **One accent, many gradients.** Flat brand blue for interactive elements; cyan-to-blue gradients (#65DDF5 → #3A6DBA) for brand backgrounds and decorative bands; a coral-to-pink gradient (#DB6E4F → #DA4E8F) reserved for headline statistics.
- **Two typefaces.** Unbounded for display headlines and large numbers; Montserrat for everything else. DM Serif Display appears only on the Velocity event page.
- **Soft geometry.** Rounded corners everywhere (8px on controls, 15–20px on cards), pill-shaped icon buttons, and diffuse shadows. No hard edges or 1px-black outlines.
- **Logo-led.** The "917V" mark (black numerals, blue V) sits top-left in a sticky white header on every page.

## Voice and copy (Verified patterns, Proposed rules)

- Headlines are short declarative statements, often uppercase section titles ("OUR PORTFOLIO COMPANIES", "PARTNER WITH US").
- Calls to action are verb-first and concise: "Learn More", "Explore 917V Life", "Partner With Us", "Send Message".
- Body copy is warm but professional; second person ("we", "you") is common.
- **Proposed for products:** keep labels sentence case in dashboards and admin tools (uppercase is a marketing-page device); use the same verb-first CTA pattern for buttons; avoid jargon in empty states and error messages (see [../design-system/components.md](../design-system/components.md#empty-states)).
